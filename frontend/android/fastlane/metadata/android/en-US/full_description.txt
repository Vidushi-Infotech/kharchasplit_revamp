KharchaSplit is the simple way to split bills with friends and keep track of your own money. Made for India: rupees, UPI and Hinglish, with groups for every trip, flat and dinner.

SPLIT EXPENSES WITH FRIENDS
Going on a trip, sharing a flat with roommates, or splitting a dinner bill? Create a group, add expenses as they happen, and KharchaSplit keeps the hisaab of who owes whom.

• Split equally, by exact amounts, by percentage or by shares
• More than one person paid? Add every payer
• Snap the bill: scan a receipt and the total is filled in for you
• Repeating expenses like rent, maid, Wi-Fi or a subscription are added automatically every month
• Simplify debts: fewer payments to settle the whole group
• Group in INR or another currency for trips abroad

SETTLE UP WITH UPI
• Pay what you owe straight from your UPI app: Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm and more
• Record cash or bank payments in one tap; balances update for everyone instantly
• Settle all your dues in a group at once
• Payment history for every friend and group

INVITE ANYONE
Add friends from your contacts. Not on KharchaSplit yet? Invite them on WhatsApp, and their share is waiting when they join.

PERSONAL MODE: YOUR OWN KHARCHA
Switch to Personal mode to manage your own money, separate from groups.
• Track daily spending and income in seconds
• Just say it: "200 for petrol yesterday from cash" and it is added for you
• Insights: see where your money goes, by category and by month
• Monthly budgets with a clear view of what is left
• Accounts: cash, bank, cards and wallets, with transfers between them
• Bills and subscriptions: reminders before they are due, mark them paid in a tap

ALSO
• Clear balances: green for money you get, red for money you owe
• Notifications when friends add expenses or pay you
• Reports and CSV export of your expenses
• Dark mode
• Works on phone, tablet and the web, synced across devices

Who is KharchaSplit for?
Friends on a Goa trip, roommates sharing rent and groceries, couples, office teams ordering lunch, families managing household expenses, students splitting hostel kharcha, and anyone who wants a simple expense tracker and budget planner.
